The model

Agents are pull-only. Nothing ever connects to a monitored host.

Every command sits in a queue on the server, HMAC-signed with a timestamp. Hosts reach out on a heartbeat, verify the signature and the clock skew, then execute. There is no inbound port to expose on an endpoint.

  • No listening port on an endpoint. Nothing to expose, nothing to firewall inbound, and roaming laptops behind NAT work unchanged.
  • Commands arrive on the next heartbeat. Worst case is about a minute at the default interval, not instant, and that trade is deliberate.
  • Clocks matter. A command older than 10 minutes or more than 60 seconds in the future is rejected, so a host with drifted time refuses everything it is sent.
  • Air-gapped runs indefinitely. The licence verifies against a local RSA-4096 signature. No check-in, no grace period to run out.

In the console

One console. The whole compliance and response surface.

Grouped the way the interface groups it: operations, investigation, network and data, administration. Every module writes to the same evidence and audit trail.

Operations

Continuous compliance

Every score decomposes into per-control, per-host outcomes with timestamps. Unreachable hosts count as not-assessed, so a partial scan reports lower, not perfect.

Operations

Evidence bundles

Export as JSON or a standalone HTML report across five scopes, with secrets redacted and the SHA-256 of every referenced artefact recorded alongside it.

Operations

Hardening

Real remediation with approval, rollback and post-change validation. A control returns to compliant only when a re-check says so, never on attempt alone.

Investigation

Digital forensics

Case management with full chain-of-custody, artefact collection, metadata and entropy analysis, YARA scanning and IOC extraction for threat hunting.

Investigation

File integrity monitoring

Continuous monitoring on every enrolled endpoint. Any add, modify or delete on a watched path generates an immutable event that surfaces in compliance posture.

Investigation

Review queue

A single analyst workflow for DLP incidents, FIM violations and firewall findings, with centralized cross-team triage and disposition.

Network and data

Network discovery

Twelve techniques including ARP, mDNS, SSDP, NetBIOS, SNMP, banner and TLS inspection, and OUI lookup, so switches, printers and appliances get identified, not listed as an IP.

Network and data

Firewall governance

Set-based shadow and duplicate detection with CIDR and port containment. Dangerous patterns like any-any and broad subnets are scored and mapped into topology.

Network and data

Data loss prevention

Pattern-based detection for credentials and PII. File-write violations are blocked inline at the agent; network, USB, clipboard and email are monitored, with evidence redacted.

Why it holds up

The sentence after the score.

Most tooling gives you a number. The hard part is the sentence after it: why that score, which control, on which host, at what time, and where is the artefact that proves it to an auditor who does not trust you.

  • Decomposable scores. Every percentage traces to real, timestamped test results, never estimates.
  • Content-hashed evidence. Bundles carry the SHA-256 of everything they reference, redacted where sensitive.
  • Validated remediation. Compliant is a state a re-check confirms, not a box someone ticked.

An honest map

Where the product actually is.

"Production ready" without qualification is not useful to anyone planning a pilot. Here is the real state.

Field-tested end to end

Linux agent across install, heartbeat, compliance scanning, FIM, DLP, command signing and signed self-upgrade, verified against real Ubuntu hosts and PostgreSQL. Network discovery and full offline licensing, verified against real environments.

Covered by tests, not yet field-tested

macOS and Windows agents are implemented and covered by the suite. Hardening has run against lab hosts only, never a production host, and is gated behind approval and rollback for exactly that reason.

Deliberately absent

Multi-tenancy. Every deployment is one organisation, which is the right trade for on-premises and the wrong one for SaaS. That decision is load-bearing, not a gap.
