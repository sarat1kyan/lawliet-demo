Why Lawliet

Any tool can give you a number. The hard part is the sentence after it: why that score, which control, on which host, at what time, and where is the proof for an auditor who will not take your word for it.

Scores that break down

Every score decomposes into per-control, per-host results with timestamps. Unreachable hosts are counted, not hidden.

Evidence that holds up

Evidence bundles export as JSON or a standalone HTML report, with secrets redacted and the SHA-256 of every artefact they reference.

Fixes that are checked

Hardening runs with approval and rollback, and a control returns to compliant only when a re-check passes, never on the attempt alone.

Platform

One console for compliance, detection and response.

Every module writes to the same evidence store and audit trail, so what you detect, fix and prove stays one record instead of four exports.

Prove compliance

Know your posture on every host, all the time.

  • Continuous compliance. Scheduled scans across the estate or selected hosts, with drift detected between scans.
  • Evidence bundles. Auditor-ready exports across scans, drift, remediation and attestations.
  • Hardening. Remediation with approval, rollback and validation on the host.
  • Scheduled reports. Dated reports per framework. Anything not scanned shows as not assessed.

Detect and respond

See what changed, decide once, act on the host.

  • SIEM. Ingests syslog, CEF, LEEF and JSON. Threshold, sequence, first-seen, went-quiet and spike rules. Forwards to Splunk, Sentinel or Sumo Logic.
  • File integrity monitoring. Hash-chained change history on watched paths, linked to the controls it affects.
  • Active defence. Quarantine a file, kill a process, block an IP, isolate a host or disable a user, on Linux, Windows and macOS.
  • Review queue. One deduplicated queue across every module, so nothing is triaged twice.

Investigate

Work a case from first signal to written finding.

  • Digital forensics. Cases with chain of custody, YARA scanning, strings, entropy and IOC extraction.
  • Asset intelligence. Inventory and criticality for every host, feeding risk scoring.
  • Audit trail. Who did what, when and with whose approval, filterable and exportable.

Network and data

Find what is on the network and what should not leave it.

  • Network discovery. Twelve techniques, from ARP and SNMP to TLS certificates and MAC vendor lookup, so switches and printers are identified, not guessed.
  • Firewall governance. Cisco ASA, FortiGate and iptables rule sets checked for any-any, shadowed and duplicate rules.
  • Data loss prevention. Credentials and personal data detected and blocked inline, with an incident workflow for analysts.

Security

Secure by design, not by configuration.

A compliance platform holds the keys to every host it watches. Lawliet is built on the assumption that someone will try to use them.

Nothing connects to your hosts

Agents are pull-only and open no listening port. They reach the server on a heartbeat, so laptops behind NAT work too.

Every command is signed

Commands carry a per-agent HMAC-SHA256 signature and a timestamp. Anything older than ten minutes is refused.

Four-eyes approval

Containment, remediation, firewall changes, user and role changes, licences and updates need a second approver. Nobody approves their own request.

Two-factor by default

TOTP is required from the first sign-in, with recovery codes and a forced change of the initial password. OIDC single sign-on is available.

Roles that match your organisation

Seven built-in roles, from administrator to auditor and viewer, plus per-user grants and denials. A denial always wins.

Signed agent upgrades

Agent releases are verified against an Ed25519 key held offline, separate from licensing. Automatic upgrade is off until you turn it on.

A complete audit trail

Every action is recorded with who made it and who approved it, and kept for two years by default.

Licensing with no phone-home

Licences are RSA-4096 signed and checked on your server. There is no licence server to reach and no telemetry.

Signed, compiled delivery

Each bundle carries a signed manifest, and the server ships compiled to native code for Intel/AMD and ARM.

Questions

What buyers ask first.

More in the documentation.

Does any of our data leave our network?

No. The platform, its database and every result stay on your server. There is no telemetry, and the licence is verified locally, so nothing needs to reach us.

Can it run fully air-gapped?

Yes. Installation, activation and updates all work from a bundle you carry in. Activation needs only a 32-character fingerprint sent to us by any channel you like.

What happens when a licence expires?

The platform pauses. Sign-in, two-factor and the licence page stay available so a renewal can be entered, and your data is untouched.

How are updates delivered?

As a new signed bundle. ./lawliet update backs up the database first and stops if that fails, keeps the previous version beside the new one and runs the migrations.

Can we keep our existing SIEM?

Yes. Lawliet forwards events as CEF, LEEF, RFC 5424 or JSON over syslog or HTTPS, including Splunk HEC, Microsoft Sentinel and Sumo Logic.

Which systems can it monitor?

Linux, Windows 10 and 11, Windows Server 2019 and 2022, and macOS, all through the agent. Network devices through discovery and syslog, and firewalls through rule-set import.

Is it multi-tenant?

No, by design. Each deployment serves one organisation, which keeps data separation simple for on-premises use. Service providers run one deployment per customer.
